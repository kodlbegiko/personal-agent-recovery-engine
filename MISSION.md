# Mission

PARE studies whether verification-grounded, risk-aware, dependency-aware minimal recovery can restore consistent persistent-agent state after ambiguous/non-atomic action outcomes without unsafe duplicate effects or false completion.

## Boundaries
- CPU-only deterministic simulator; no paid APIs.
- No real email, calendar, finance, commerce, cloud deletion, or physical-device actions.
- Ground truth is machine-testable simulator oracle state.
- No human labels or preferences are required for the primary claims.
- Claims are benchmark-specific unless independently validated elsewhere.
